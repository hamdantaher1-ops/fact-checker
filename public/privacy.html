Privacy Policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Fat Checker is a small, independent tool that looks up an Instagram post you link to and researches whether the product or service in it is worth buying. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

What you give the app

  • The Instagram post link you paste in.
  • Nothing else. There's no account, no sign-up, and no personal profile.

What the app collects automatically

  • The post's caption text — read directly from the Instagram post you linked to, so the app knows what product or service to research.
  • Your IP address — used only to enforce a daily limit on how many researches one visitor can run, so the app doesn't get overwhelmed or abused. It is not logged alongside anything else about you, and it isn't used to identify or track you across visits beyond that daily count.

Who else sees this

To do its job, the app sends the information above to two outside services:

  • Meta (Instagram's parent company), to fetch basic public information about the post you linked to, through Instagram's own official lookup endpoint.
  • Anthropic, whose AI model reads the caption and searches the web to produce the verdict you see. The caption text is sent to Anthropic for this purpose; nothing else about you is sent.

No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. There are no analytics trackers or ad networks on this site.

What's stored

The app doesn't keep a database of your searches. Each request is processed and the result is shown to you in the moment; it isn't saved afterward, except for a short-lived daily counter (tied to your IP address, described above) used purely for the rate limit, which clears itself after about a day.

About the verdicts

Verdicts are generated automatically by an AI model researching public information. They are opinions formed from that research, not professional, legal, or financial advice, and they can be wrong. Always use your own judgment before purchasing anything.

Changes to this policy

If how the app handles data changes in a meaningful way, this page will be updated and the date at the top will reflect that.
